What one detection becomes

Six steps from a camera frame to a verified saving. Select a step to open it.

A camera sees an activity

Detector and tracker output a structured event: type, camera, duration, confidence.

The ledger prices it

Activity × emission factor × grid intensity, tagged Scope 1, 2 or 3, with the factor and source kept.

The twin tests fixes

Monte Carlo runs give a range of tonnes saved, rupees saved and payback, not one false-precise number.

The advisor picks a plan

An optimiser selects actions within budget; a language model explains the plan for the reader’s role.

You act and verify

Predicted savings are compared with measured ones so the model can be corrected.

Export for reporting

Ledger CSV and a summary report, organised by GHG Protocol scopes.

How it works

The full platform design, and exactly which parts run in this demo.

1 IngestCameras, drones, smart meters, BMS, ERP, weather, grid intensity. Kafka, MQTT, Airflow.
2 VisionYOLOv8 or RT-DETR detects; ByteTrack tracks; activity becomes a carbon event.
3 AccountingActivity × factor × grid intensity, Scope 1/2/3, versioned factors, audit trail.
4 TwinAsset graph, scenario simulation, Monte Carlo, ROI and payback.
5 AdvisorRAG over policies and factors, optimiser, role-aware plan, verification loop.

Privacy and trust

Faces and licence plates are blurred at the edge before any frame leaves the site. Every number in the ledger keeps its factor, version and source. High-impact recommendations go through human review. The language model never invents figures; it explains numbers the optimiser produced.

How success is measured

Detection accuracy (mAP), tonnes identified versus tonnes verified, recommendation adoption, simulation error against real outcomes, event-to-insight latency, and cost per tonne CO₂e saved.
